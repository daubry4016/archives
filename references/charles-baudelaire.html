CHARLES BAUDELAIRE

1821 — 1867
poet · critic · translator

biography

Charles Baudelaire was a French poet and literary critic whose work transformed the possibilities of modern poetry. He is widely regarded as one of the central figures of French modernity.

His writing moves between beauty and decay, pleasure and boredom, idealism and the darker aspects of modern life. He was fascinated by the city, fleeting encounters, artificial beauty and the strange poetry hidden within ordinary experience.

works

Les Fleurs du mal
A collection exploring beauty, desire, mortality, decadence, boredom and the tension between the ideal and the modern world.
Le Spleen de Paris
Prose poems inspired by modern urban life, capturing fleeting impressions, loneliness and the contradictions of the city.
The Painter of Modern Life
A collection of reflections on art, fashion, modernity and the artist's relationship with contemporary life.
Artificial Paradises
A series of reflections on intoxication, imagination and altered states of consciousness.
