# philosophies

> *questions without final answers.*

---

### Existentialism

Questions of freedom, meaning, responsibility and the individual's place in an indifferent world.

### Nihilism

The questioning of inherent meaning, value and purpose.

### Stoicism

The distinction between what lies within our control and what does not.

### Absurdism

The tension between humanity's search for meaning and an indifferent universe.

### Platonism

Ideas, forms, knowledge and the distinction between appearance and reality.

### Aristotelianism

Logic, virtue, causality and the pursuit of human flourishing.

### Phenomenology

The study of experience and consciousness as they are lived.

### Ethics

Questions concerning morality, responsibility, virtue and how one ought to live.

### Metaphysics

Questions concerning existence, reality, identity, causation and what lies beyond the physical.

---

*the questions remain.*
