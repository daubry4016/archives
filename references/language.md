# language

> *words carry what silence cannot.*

---

### Latin

A language of ancient texts, philosophy, theology and inscriptions.

> *carpe diem*
> *memento mori*
> *amor fati*

### French

A language of poetry, literature and elegance.

> *c'est la vie*
> *à jamais*
> *l'esprit*

### Italian

The language of Dante, poetry and the literary tradition of the Renaissance.

> *nel mezzo del cammin*
> *dolce stil novo*

### Ancient Greek

A language deeply connected to philosophy, mythology and classical literature.

> *γνῶθι σεαυτόν*
> *πάντα ῥεῖ*
