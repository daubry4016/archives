DANTE ALIGHIERI

1265 — 1321
poet · philosopher · political thinker

biography

Dante Alighieri was an Italian poet and thinker whose work became one of the foundations of Western literary tradition. Born in Florence in 1265, he lived through political conflict, exile and profound personal loss.

His writing brought together poetry, philosophy, theology, politics and questions of human nature. His imagined journey through the afterlife became not only a vision of Hell, Purgatory and Paradise, but also a reflection on morality, justice, love and the possibility of transformation.

works

La Divina Commedia
A journey through Inferno, Purgatorio and Paradiso, combining theology, philosophy, politics and poetry.
Vita Nuova
A collection of prose and poetry centered around love, memory and Dante's idealized figure of Beatrice.
Convivio
An unfinished philosophical work exploring knowledge, virtue, love and the nature of human understanding.
De Monarchia
A political and philosophical treatise concerning authority, justice and the relationship between temporal and spiritual power.
