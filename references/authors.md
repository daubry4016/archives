# authors

> *voices that left something behind.*

---

### Dante Alighieri

*Divine Comedy · Vita Nuova*

A poet whose work shaped the language of love, exile, morality and the afterlife.

### Friedrich Nietzsche

*Thus Spoke Zarathustra · Beyond Good and Evil*

Philosophy, morality, suffering, creation and the individual.

### Franz Kafka

*The Metamorphosis · The Trial*

Alienation, absurdity, bureaucracy and the strange weight of existence.

### Fyodor Dostoevsky

*Crime and Punishment · The Brothers Karamazov*

Faith, morality, guilt, freedom and the contradictions of human nature.

### Edgar Allan Poe

*The Raven · The Tell-Tale Heart*

Darkness, obsession, melancholy and the macabre.

### Virginia Woolf

*Mrs Dalloway · To the Lighthouse*

Memory, consciousness, time and the interior life.
### Albert Camus

*The Stranger · The Myth of Sisyphus*

Absurdity, freedom, rebellion and the search for meaning
in a world that offers none.

### Arthur Schopenhauer

*The World as Will and Representation*

Desire, suffering, pessimism and the restless nature of existence.

### Charles Baudelaire

*Les Fleurs du mal*

Beauty, decadence, desire, melancholy and the strange poetry of ruin.

### Søren Kierkegaard

*Either/Or · Fear and Trembling*

Anxiety, faith, choice and the difficulty of becoming oneself.

### Fernando Pessoa

*The Book of Disquiet · Mensagem*

Identity, solitude, multiplicity and the restless interior life.
