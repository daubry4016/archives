FERNANDO PESSOA

1888 — 1935
poet · writer · translator

biography

Fernando Pessoa was a Portuguese poet and writer whose work is distinguished by its extraordinary multiplicity of voices, styles and literary identities.

Rather than writing under a single stable artistic identity, Pessoa created numerous heteronyms with distinct biographies, personalities, philosophies and literary styles. His work repeatedly returns to questions of identity, consciousness, perception and the distance between the self and the world.

works

The Book of Disquiet
A fragmented work of reflections, observations and meditations on identity, solitude, consciousness and ordinary existence.
Mensagem
A collection of poems drawing on Portuguese history, mythology and national symbolism.
Poetry of Alberto Caeiro
Poetry centered on direct perception, nature and the rejection of excessive abstraction.
Poetry of Álvaro de Campos
Intense and restless poetry concerned with modernity, sensation, technology, identity and existential unease.

heteronyms

Alberto Caeiro
A poet of nature and immediate perception, often presented as a rejection of metaphysical speculation.
Ricardo Reis
A classical and stoic voice concerned with restraint, fate, mortality and the passing of time.
Álvaro de Campos
A restless modernist voice characterized by intensity, contradiction, sensation and existential anxiety.
