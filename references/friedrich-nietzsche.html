FRIEDRICH NIETZSCHE

1844 — 1900
philosopher · writer · philologist

biography

Friedrich Nietzsche was a German philosopher and writer whose work challenged conventional ideas of morality, religion, truth and human nature.

His writing often took the form of aphorisms, philosophical narratives and poetic prose. Rather than offering a single system of thought, Nietzsche repeatedly questioned the values inherited from tradition and asked what it might mean to create new ones.

works

Thus Spoke Zarathustra
A philosophical and poetic work exploring transformation, self-overcoming, the Übermensch and the eternal recurrence.
Beyond Good and Evil
A critique of traditional morality, philosophical dogmatism and assumptions about truth and value.
On the Genealogy of Morality
An investigation into the historical development of moral concepts such as guilt, punishment and responsibility.
The Birth of Tragedy
An early work examining Greek tragedy, art and the tension between Apollonian and Dionysian forces.

related

Nihilism
Nietzsche examined the crisis of meaning produced by the collapse of inherited values, while resisting nihilism as a final destination.
Existentialism
Though Nietzsche predates existentialism as a movement, his questions concerning freedom, meaning, values and self-creation profoundly influenced later thinkers.
Friedrich Nietzsche & Dante
Two very different approaches to morality, suffering, transformation and the meaning of human existence.
